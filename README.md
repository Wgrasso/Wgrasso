# Hi, I'm Willem Grasso

Clinical Technology student @ TU Delft, building in neurotechnology and digital health.

I like the part of medicine where the engineering has to survive contact with a real
patient, a real clinician, or a real family. Two of my apps are on the App Store; the
rest is research that had to hold up to a baseline.

### Health and research

- **[HiNikki](https://github.com/Wgrasso/hinikki-overview)** — a voice companion for older
  adults living with dementia, plus the family app that keeps its world true. On TestFlight.
  Public architecture overview; source is private.
- **[PPG Predictor](https://github.com/Wgrasso/pvp-predictor)** — the app from my bachelor
  research at Erasmus MC: predicting portal pressure after a TIPS procedure from CT anatomy.
  Two models, every coefficient visible and editable. The honest finding was that CT anatomy
  alone isn't enough — that's in the README, along with the full study.
- **[tDCS and post-stroke fatigue](https://github.com/Wgrasso/tdcs-post-stroke-fatigue-meta-analysis)**
  — systematic review and meta-analysis.
- **[ICU artifact detection](https://github.com/Wgrasso/ic-artifact-detection)** — finding
  artifacts in intensive care monitoring signals.

### Shipped to the App Store

- **[Happie](https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/happie/id6757129676)** — group meal planning without
  the endless group chat: vote on what to cook, see who's eating, keep the recipes you liked.
  Over 500 downloads. [More about it](https://willemgrasso.com/projects/happie.html).
- **[Corvator](https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/corvator/id6759965966)** — chore enforcement for
  student houses. I live with sixteen people; the dishes are a scheduling problem with a
  social cost, so I made the cost legible. Tally marks, a first-in-first-out penalty queue,
  and a Sunday session that picks four names.
