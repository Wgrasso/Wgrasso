# Hi, I'm Willem Grasso

Clinical Technology student at TU Delft, building in neurotechnology and digital health.

I like the part of medicine where the engineering has to survive contact with a real
patient, a real clinician, or a real family. Two of my apps are on the App Store. The
rest is research that had to hold up against a baseline.

**Next step:** MSc Biomedical Engineering (Neurotechnology).

## Health and research

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| **[HiNikki](https://github.com/Wgrasso/hinikki-overview)** | A voice companion for older adults living with dementia, plus the family app that keeps its world true. On TestFlight. Public architecture overview; the source stays private. |
| **[PPG Predictor](https://github.com/Wgrasso/pvp-predictor)** | My bachelor research at Erasmus MC: predicting portal pressure after a TIPS procedure from CT anatomy. Two models, every coefficient visible and editable. The honest finding was that CT anatomy alone is not enough, which is in the README along with the full study. |
| **[tDCS and post-stroke fatigue](https://github.com/Wgrasso/tdcs-post-stroke-fatigue-meta-analysis)** | Systematic review and meta-analysis. |
| **[ICU artifact detection](https://github.com/Wgrasso/ic-artifact-detection)** | Finding artifacts in intensive care monitoring signals. |
